# Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

### Microsoft Excel | Business Analytics Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

This independent portfolio project analyses **9,994 Superstore transaction records from 2014–2017** to evaluate sales trends, profitability across product categories and regions, and the relationship between discounting and financial performance.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through data preparation, exploratory analysis, and dashboard visualisation.

## Tools & Techniques

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis and dashboard development
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation
- **PivotTables:** Data aggregation and performance analysis
- **PivotCharts:** Visualisation of sales and profitability patterns
- **KPIs:** Tracking overall sales, profit, and profit margin

## Analytical Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:** Cleaned and transformed the dataset using Power Query, ensuring appropriate data types and retaining valid transaction records.
2. **Exploratory Analysis:** Used PivotTables to examine monthly sales trends, category and regional profit contributions, and discount-related profitability.
3. **Dashboard Development:** Built an Excel dashboard integrating key performance indicators and interactive visualisations.
4. **Business Interpretation:** Identified performance gaps and developed proposed recommendations based on the findings.

## Key Findings

**1. Discounting & Profitability**

Transactions discounted above 20% collectively recorded approximately **$135K in losses**, raising questions about the financial effectiveness of aggressive discounting.

**2. Category Profitability**

Technology contributed approximately **$145K in profit**, compared with **$18K from Furniture**, indicating substantial differences in profit contributions across product categories.

**3. Regional Performance**

The West region generated approximately **$108K in profit**, compared with **$40K from the Central region**, highlighting opportunities for further investigation into regional performance differences.

## Proposed Business Recommendations

- **Profit-Based Discount Controls:** Evaluate product-level profitability before approving high-discount promotions.
- **Category Profitability Improvement:** Identify weaker-performing Furniture products and assess targeted pricing or promotional adjustments.
- **Regional Performance Optimisation:** Compare product mix and discounting practices across regions and test suitable improvement opportunities.

*These recommendations are proposed analytical outcomes and have not been implemented or validated through business operations.*

## Project Deliverables

- **Excel Workbook:** Cleaned dataset, PivotTables, and business performance dashboard.
- **Portfolio Presentation:** Five-slide PDF summarizing the project, dashboard, key findings, and recommendations.
